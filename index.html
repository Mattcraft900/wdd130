My Favorite Projects

Web Development Society

As a member of the BYU-I CSE Forum & Web Development Society, I have the awesome opportunity to work as part of a medium-sized team in developing a real-world website for a local organization. We are currently working with Undergrad, which is a small organization that manages, hosts, and provides infrastructure for community-level races (like 5k's or 10k's) that are open to the public. I have personally contributed to ony a few pages at this point (including the placeholder Testimonials section, pictured here), but the experience with real-world applications (pun not intended) has been invaluable to me. I'm very proud of it.

This Portal Page!

Hopefully, this personal portfolio website will be relatively underwhelming compared to some of the stuff that I go on to create/contribute to in the future, but at the time of writing this, it's easily the largest complete project that I've made on my own -- even if it is pretty simple. This page, in combination with the whitewater rafting and class "commission" assignments, will forever hold a special place in my heart for giving me confidence in my burgeoning web development skills & unlocking a love for the art in me during my college career!

D&D Character Database

I had always heard the name "SQL" (pronounce that however you will) get tossed around in the software development sphere, so finally coming to understand what it is and why it's important felt like a huge milestone to me. Throughout the class we were required to build, populate, and maintain (and re-build, re-populate, keep maintaining, etc.) a functional SQL database of our own. I chose to construct a database that could keep track of D&D characters using the base SRD ruleset. Pictured here is the ER Diagram of the mostly-complete database. Truth be told, I would have liked to expand the scope of the project to include a multitude of other things like items, multiclassing, etc., but it's porbably for my own good that the project limited us to a max of 10-12 tables, haha.
